🎯 Four in a Row · Initial P

Say the /p/ word, then drop your counter. First to four in a row wins!

How to play

  1. Cut out the counters at the bottom (or use coins/buttons).
  2. Players take turns. On your turn, pick a column.
  3. Say the word in the lowest empty circle of that column out loud — pop your lips for a clear P.
  4. If your therapist/partner says it's a good P, place your counter in that circle.
  5. First player to get four in a row — across, down, or diagonally — wins!

Variations

  • Easy: say the word once. Harder: say it 3× or use it in a sentence.
  • Use real Connect 4 counters and place this sheet under the frame.
  • Play to five in a row for a longer game.
  • Cover counters with sticky tack to remove and replay.
