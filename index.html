About Yapla

Yapla makes warm, practical resources for building communication — from single-sound picture cards and minimal-pair flashcards to vocabulary, sentence and social-language activities. Every set is designed to print cleanly for the clinic, the classroom or the kitchen table, and to keep the target sound or word the clear star of the page.

Card pictures are Microsoft's Fluent Emoji, used under the MIT licence so they look the same on every device and print.
