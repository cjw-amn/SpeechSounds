🚀 Rocket Race · Initial P

Blast off from the launch pad, roll, move, and say the /p/ word — first rocket to Planet P wins!

How to play

  1. Print both sheets. The flight-path board is on page 2.
  2. Each player puts a counter on the 🚀 LAUNCH pad.
  3. Roll the dice and move your counter that many spaces along the flight path.
  4. Say the word you land on out loud — pop your lips for a strong P.
  5. If it's a good P, you stay. If not, slide back one space and try that word.
  6. First player to reach 🪐 PLANET P at the end wins the race!

Make it harder

  • Say the word 3 times, or use it in a sentence.
  • You must land on Planet P with an exact roll.
  • Name another P word that rhymes or starts the same.
  • Play solo: beat your own time around the track.

🎲 Dice — fold & tape

Cut the cross out, fold on the lines, tape into a cube. Or just use any dice you have.

🚀 Counters — cut out

Cut out a rocket counter each (or use coins / buttons). Up to 8 players.

▶ Game board on the next sheet — follow the dashed flight line from 🚀 Launch to 🪐 Planet P.
